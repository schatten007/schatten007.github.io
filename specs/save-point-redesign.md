# SAVE POINT / SCHATTEN

## User direction

Anime, games, RPGs, Doom and other old-school shooters, and PC/laptop tuning. Earlier feedback still applies: readable text, natural copy, and understandable lab results.

## Design decisions

- A game-manual / character-loadout interface: heavy condensed lettering, clipped panel corners, rivets, inventory slots, mission numbers, and equipment illustrations.
- Bone, gunmetal, vermilion, amber, and muted mint. Light reading pages and dark equipment panels share the same tokens.
- An original cel-shaded mechanic avatar, explicitly an illustration. No fictional claims about the owner's appearance, equipment, achievements, or skill levels.
- Three selectable character loadouts expose real interests: AI/code, hardware, and games/anime.
- Projects remain named projects with public repositories and dated evidence. The mission styling changes their presentation, not their completion status.
- The lab gains an interactive power-budget model. Its formula and limits are shown; outputs are illustrative indices, not hardware measurements.
- Body copy is 18px desktop / 17px mobile. Controls are at least 16px with 44px targets. Non-decorative metadata is at least 13px.
- Navigation remains plain-language Projects, Lab, Notes, About. Every reading route works without JavaScript.

## Copy review

1. Remove unsupported anecdotes from the earlier rewrite (personal failures, forgetting habits, full-time schedule, and precise client responsibilities).
2. Use supplied career facts and user-confirmed interests. Preserve source attribution and benchmark scope.
3. Read each page as a visitor: identify the owner, understand each project's purpose, find the code, and tell a demonstration from an actual measurement.

## Acceptance checks

- The home page is visibly a new composition with original artwork, inventory controls, and a real project-selection section.
- Loadout controls work with keyboard and touch, and announce the changed description.
- Power-budget controls show the expected values, including the cooling cap; reset returns both controls to the documented baseline.
- The schema demo explains its intentionally broken input before running; exit 0, 1, and 2 remain distinct.
- Primary pages have no horizontal overflow at 360px, 390px, tablet, and desktop widths.
- Core reading text and primary controls meet the size floor; WCAG AA color checks pass.
- Reduced-motion settings disable ambient motion, and the manual preference persists.
- The PDF and social preview match the updated content and design.
- GitHub Actions checks and publishes successfully; the public home and lab are smoke-tested.
